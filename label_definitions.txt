# Definitions copied from README.md for the baseline.

analysis: An F1 interpretation or prediction that connects its conclusion to a specific, checkable event, quote, comparison, or number offered as support.
hot_take: An F1 judgment about performance or strategy, prediction, or proposed strategic action that does not connect its conclusion to a specific, checkable supporting detail, even if the claim may be true.
question: A comment primarily asking for an answerable F1 fact, explanation, or source while withholding its own conclusion.
reaction: A comment primarily reporting or responding to one F1 moment with a fact, feeling, joke, or cheer, without advancing a broader judgment or seeking an answer.
